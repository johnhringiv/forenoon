Feeds blogs and sites, by address

Add a feed

Paste a feed's address, or just the site's: the feed is looked for and the address corrected within a minute. "Best of page and feed" takes whichever copy of an article is fuller; "Web page only" suits feeds that carry just a teaser; "Feed only" never fetches the article page. "Places a day" is the most of an issue the source may take (0 for no limit); links you add by hand do not count. A pinned source's articles are all taken, before all others, and past its places a day they are extra: they do not use the day's places. A feed is yours alone until it is shared: then the rest of the household can follow it too. Changes apply from the next run.

How to get the cookie, and what is done with it

Sign in to the site in your browser and either export its cookies as a header string with an extension such as Cookie-Editor, or open the developer tools, pick any request to the site on the Network tab and copy the value of its Cookie request header. Saving a site again replaces its cookie. It is kept where only the browser container can read it, never shown again, and sent to that site only. If it stops working, or the site asks for a CAPTCHA, it says so here and by email, and picks from the site are shown as links until it is replaced.
