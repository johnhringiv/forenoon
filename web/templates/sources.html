Feeds blogs and sites, by address

Paste a feed's address, or just the site's: the feed is looked for and the address corrected within a minute. "Best of page and feed" takes whichever copy of an article is fuller; "Web page only" suits feeds that carry just a teaser; "Feed only" never fetches the article page. "Places a day" is the most of an issue the source may take (0 for no limit); links you add by hand do not count. A pinned source's articles are all taken, before all others, and past its places a day they are extra: they do not use the day's places. Changes apply from the next run.

To get the cookie, sign in to the site in your browser and either export its cookies as a header string with an extension such as Cookie-Editor, or open the developer tools, pick any request to the site on the Network tab and copy the value of its Cookie request header. Saving a site again replaces its cookie. It is kept where only the browser container can read it, never shown again, and sent to that site only.
